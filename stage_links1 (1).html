Full sibling group confirmed: Moïse Papineau (1856–1947) and Marie Georgiana Sauvé (m. 22 October 1878, St. Simon & St. Jude, Belle River) had thirteen children: Marie Sarah, Rebecca Anne, Marie Eloize, Adelaine, Régina, Moïse Jr., Joseph Alfred Antoine (died in infancy), Joseph Réné, Marie Georgiana, Théodore Paul, Marie Lelia, Joseph Damien "Udule/Udle", and Wilfred (b. 1880–1903). Joseph Réné's line extends three further generations in Tecumseh, Ontario. Note: Marie Georgiana's Sauvé surname is shared with, but not confirmed related to, the separate Sauvé branch on this tree.

    Sources & Research Guide

    This tree separates record references named in the working family information from entries that currently rely only on family-supplied notes. Select any person in the tree to see the sources connected to that profile. Click a source link to open the archive or record collection in a new tab.

    Important: A record named here is a research lead unless an image, archive call number, database citation, or full transcription has been attached. Dashed person cards and “Needs documentary confirmation” labels should not be treated as proven relationships.

    Record groups cited in the working information

    • Reformed Protestant registers of Lintot, Normandy, for Testard family baptisms and the 1627 and 1630 marriages.
      Open Seine-Maritime Protestant registers ↗
    • La Rochelle record dated 26 June 1651 concerning Jean, Jacques, and Charles Testard.
      Open Charente-Maritime archives ↗
    • Québec Catholic parish registers, including Notre-Dame de Montréal, St-Antoine-de-la-Rivière-du-Loup, Yamaska, and St-François-du-Lac.
      Search Québec Catholic parish registers ↗
    • Ontario Catholic parish registers, including L’Assomption in Windsor and St-Simon & Jude in Belle River.
      Search Ontario Catholic church records ↗
    • Canadian census records that can help verify Ontario and Québec households.
      Search Library and Archives Canada censuses ↗
    • Family-provided information for recent Papineau–Sauvé descendants and living generations. Private family information has no public web link.

    About these links: They open the archive or searchable collection connected to the citation. They do not always open the exact scanned page because image numbers and permanent record links were not included in the supplied notes. A free FamilySearch sign-in may be required for some images.

    Research approach

    Records for siblings, aunts, uncles and cousins were used as supporting evidence to confirm parentage, surname variants, dates and migration. Those collateral branches are not displayed in the tree.

    Still open: Clara’s maiden surname and parents, original-record confirmation for the earlier Sauvé bridge, and several Ontario citations. Unconfirmed information remains dashed and is not presented as certain.

    How source labels work

    • Record cited: the working notes name a parish, register, date, ship, or other identifiable record group.
    • Working notes: the statement is present in the supplied family chart, but no precise document citation is currently attached.

    Recommended citation details to add later

    For each original record, add the archive or website, collection title, parish and record type, event date, image or page number, record number, and the date it was accessed. This will make every conclusion independently checkable.
